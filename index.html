Privacy Policy — Med Reminder

Last updated: May 15, 2026  |  Effective date: May 15, 2026

Thank you for using Med Reminder ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Medication data: Names, dosages, schedules, and recurrence settings you enter to set reminders.
  • Check-in records: Times and statuses (on-time, missed, snoozed) when you log medication intake.
  • Photo check-ins: Optional photos you take to confirm medication intake. Photos are stored locally on your device only.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymized usage data such as:
    • Screen views and feature usage (e.g., "check_in," "add_med")
    • Crash reports and performance metrics
    • Device information (OS version, screen size, locale)
  • Advertising data: Our third-party ad partner (Google AdMob) may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your medication data on any external server.
  • We do not require account registration or email.
  • We do not share your personal health information with any third party.

How We Store Your Data

Local Storage Only

All your medication data, check-in history, and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted storage. We do not operate any cloud backend or external database.

Backup & Restore

  • JSON Export/Import: You can manually export and import your data as a JSON file. This file is stored on your device's local storage and is never sent to us.
  • CSV Export: Check-in history can be exported as CSV for personal record-keeping.

Notifications & Alarms

  • We schedule exact alarms on your device to send medication reminders at the times you specify.
  • On Android 12+, we request the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission to ensure timely delivery.
  • Notifications include your medication name, dose, and scheduled time.

Camera & Photos

  • The photo check-in feature uses CameraX to capture photos directly within the app.
  • Photos are saved to your app-private directory and are never uploaded or transmitted.
  • Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA) is requested only when you choose to use photo check-in.

Payments & Subscriptions

In-App Purchase (Pro Upgrade)

  • We use Google Play Billing to process a one-time purchase for the Pro upgrade.
  • Payment and transaction data are handled by Google Play. We receive only a purchase token to verify your Pro status — we do not see your credit card or payment details.
  • Pro status is tied to your Google Play account and can be restored on new devices.

Rewarded Ads

  • Free users may watch a rewarded advertisement to temporarily unlock Pro features for 6 hours.
  • Ad content is provided by Google AdMob. Ad tracking and personalization are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally using Android's secure storage mechanisms.
  • We use HTTPS only for analytics and ad SDK communications.
  • We recommend using your device's built-in screen lock for additional protection of your medication data.

Data Deletion

To delete all your data:

  1. Open Med Reminder → Settings → Backup Section → "Clear All Data"
  2. Or uninstall the app entirely — all local data will be removed.

Note: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all medication data, check-in history, and preferences. Please export a backup before uninstalling.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (we hold none on our servers)
  • Delete your data (clear it from the app or uninstall)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization)
  • Request a copy of your data (use the JSON backup export feature)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
